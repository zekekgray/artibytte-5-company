About ArtiBytte

One studio. One foundation. A growing family of products.

ArtiBytte builds connected software for businesses that have outgrown spreadsheets and disconnected tools — from sports academies to construction sites, kept together by one shared foundation underneath.

See the work Join the team

Why we exist.

Most businesses run on a patchwork of spreadsheets, messaging apps, and tools that don't talk to each other.

ArtiBytte started with a simple frustration: every business we looked at — an academy, a food brand, a construction crew — was running critical operations through tools that were never meant to work together. A spreadsheet for inventory. A messaging thread for approvals. A notebook for maintenance schedules.

So we started building the connective layer instead — single, focused systems that solve one business's real problem first, on a shared foundation that lets every new product move faster than the last. ArtiPlay came out of a sports academy that needed one place for rosters and dues. ArtiStock came out of a kitchen that needed to know its real cost per item. Each product still carries the name of the problem it was built to solve.

That's still how we build today — under the ATH•NA and BlockPingg brand umbrella, as part of the wider HOTS•R ecosystem.

We don't start with the technology. We start with the business, and build the system it actually needs.

— How we approach every product

What we believe.

A few principles that shape every product we ship, regardless of industry.

Specialized on the outside.

Every product is designed around the language and workflow of the business it serves — an academy shouldn't feel like a spreadsheet, and a construction site shouldn't feel like a to-do app.

Connected underneath.

Different products, same foundation. Identity, data and AI capabilities are shared across the ecosystem, so a new product doesn't mean starting from zero.

Built lean, built to run.

Many of our products are single-file, self-contained applications backed by lightweight infrastructure — fast to ship, easy to maintain, and cheap to run.

Intelligence as a layer, not a gimmick.

AI shows up as an assistant that flags what needs attention — a missed payment, a part due for service — not as a feature bolted on for its own sake.

Leadership.

Founder-led, product-first.
